### 🔬 Research Focus

I am a Computer Science researcher working at the intersection of **Deep Learning**, **Low-Resource Natural Language Processing**, and **Remote Sensing Image Processing**. My research focuses on:
- **Low-Resource NLP & e-Governance**: Intent classification, NER slot filling, and RAG pipelines for domain-specific document generation in Bangla using fine-tuned Transformer models (BanglaBERT).
- **Hyperspectral Image Processing**: Subspace detection, dimensionality reduction, few-shot learning, and convolutional neural network architectures for complex spatial-spectral classification.
